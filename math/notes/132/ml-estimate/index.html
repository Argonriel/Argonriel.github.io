thmcomplex-analysis/integration

Length estimate

If γ is piecewise C1, f is continuous on its image, |f|≤M there and L=L(γ), then |∫γf(z)dz|≤∫γ|f(z)||dz|≤ML.

Proof: apply the triangle inequality to ∫abf(γ(t))γ′(t)dt.

Sharp example: on |z|=R traversed once counterclockwise, f(z)=1/z has M=1/R, L=2πR, and integral 2πi. Equality holds in the estimate.

If continuous fn→f uniformly on a fixed path image, then |∫γfndz−∫γfdz|≤L(γ)supz∈γ|fn(z)−f(z)|→0. This is the contour version of uniform convergence allowing integration through a limit.

Boundary: the path and the bound must match. A holomorphic logarithm branch cannot be chosen continuously around an entire circle enclosing 0. An expression involving principal Log on that circle needs a cut and an explicit piecewise interpretation; do not treat it as one continuous branch along a closed loop.
