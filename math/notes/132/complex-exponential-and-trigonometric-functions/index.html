MATH 132 · Complex Analysis

Complex exponential and trigonometric functions

defcomplex-analysis/basics

ez=∑n=0∞znn!,ex+iy=ex(cos⁡y+isin⁡y). The series has infinite radius of convergence, so ez is entire and (ez)′=ez.

ez+w=ezew,|ez|=eRe⁡z,ez≠0. Also ez=ew iff z−w∈2πiZ. Thus the exponential is locally invertible everywhere but is not globally injective on C.

Complex sine and cosine

sin⁡z=eiz−e−iz2i=∑n=0∞(−1)nz2n+1(2n+1)!, cos⁡z=eiz+e−iz2=∑n=0∞(−1)nz2n(2n)!.

Both are entire, with (sin⁡z)′=cos⁡z, (cos⁡z)′=−sin⁡z and sin2⁡z+cos2⁡z=1.

Boundary: the real-variable bounds do not extend to C. For real y, cos⁡(iy)=e−y+ey2, which is unbounded as |y|→∞.
