MATH 132 · Complex Analysis

Liouville's theorem

thmcomplex-analysis/holomorphic

Liouville

A bounded entire function is constant.

Proof: if |f|≤M on all of C, Cauchy estimates give |f′(a)|≤Mr for every a∈C and every r>0. Let r→∞, then use f′≡0 on the connected plane.

Consequences:

  • There is no holomorphic bijection from C onto the unit disk: such a map would be bounded and therefore constant.
  • A nonconstant entire function has dense image in C. If its image missed a disk D(w,ε), then 1/(f−w) would be bounded and entire, forcing f to be constant.

Boundary: boundedness on a proper domain is insufficient; f(z)=z is bounded and nonconstant on the unit disk. Dense image also does not mean surjectivity: ez omits 0.
