Harmonic mean value

If u is harmonic on an open set containing D(a,r)―, then u(a)=12π∫02πu(a+reiθ)dθ.

The same identity holds componentwise for complex-valued harmonic functions, hence for holomorphic functions.

Proof idea: for 0<s≤r, let A(s)=12π∫02πu(a+seiθ)dθ. Differentiate under the integral. Green's theorem gives 2πsA′(s)=∫∂D(a,s)(−uydx+uxdy)=∬D(a,s)Δudxdy=0. Thus A(s) is constant; continuity and s↓0 give A(s)=u(a).

Integrating the circle formula in the radius also gives the disk average u(a)=1πr2∬D(a,r)u(x,y)dxdy.

Use: if u≤M near a and u(a)=M, the average can equal M only if u=M on each sufficiently small circle. This is the local step behind the maximum principle.
