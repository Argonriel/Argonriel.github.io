MATH 132 · Complex Analysis

Riemann sphere

defcomplex-analysis/basics

Extended complex plane

C^=C∪{∞}. Neighborhoods of ∞ contain {z:|z|>R}∪{∞} for some R>0.

Stereographic projection identifies this space with the unit sphere in R3. The inverse image of z=x+iy is (2x1+|z|2,2y1+|z|2,|z|2−11+|z|2). The north pole (0,0,1) corresponds to ∞; in the other direction, z=X+iY1−Z(Z≠1).

As |z|→∞, the displayed point tends to the north pole. The coordinate w=1/z, with w=0 at ∞, turns a neighborhood of infinity into a neighborhood of 0.

Notation: use C^ for the sphere and C∖{0} for the punctured plane. They are different spaces. The symbol ∞ is an added point, not a complex number on which ordinary field arithmetic always applies.
