MATH 115A · Linear Algebra I

Rank-nullity theorem

thmlinear-algebra/maps

Rank-nullity

If T:V→W is linear and dim⁡V=n<∞, then dim⁡ker⁡T+dim⁡im⁡T=n.

There is no need for the whole codomain W to be finite-dimensional.

Proof sketch: extend a basis (u1,…,uk) of ker⁡T to a basis (u1,…,uk,v1,…,vn−k) of V. Then T(v1),…,T(vn−k) span the image. A relation among them puts ∑ajvj in the kernel, forcing all aj=0.

For A∈Mm×n(F), rank⁡A+nullity⁡A=n, rank⁡AT+nullity⁡AT=m. The dimension on the right is the number of input coordinates.

If dim⁡V=dim⁡W<∞, then injective, surjective and invertible are equivalent for T:V→W. Finite dimension of V alone is not enough when the codomain has a different dimension: x↦(x,0) from R to R2 is injective but not surjective.
