MATH 115A · Linear Algebra I

Basis and dimension

defthmlinear-algebra/foundations

Basis

A basis β=(v1,…,vn) of V is an independent spanning list. Every v∈V has a unique representation v=∑j=1najvj,[v]β=(a1,…,an)T.

The coordinate map v↦[v]β is an isomorphism V→Fn. Coordinates depend on the ordered basis.

For a finite-dimensional space, all bases have the same length, called dim⁡V. The zero space has the empty basis and dimension 0.

Basis extension: an independent list can be extended to a basis; a finite spanning list can be reduced to a basis. Consequently, in an n-dimensional space:

  • an independent list has at most n vectors;
  • a spanning list has at least n vectors;
  • a list of exactly n vectors is a basis iff it is independent, iff it spans.

Proof idea: when a spanning list is dependent, remove a redundant vector. To extend an independent list, keep adding a vector outside its span; finite dimension forces the process to stop.

For Pm(F)={p:deg⁡p≤m}∪{0}, (1,x,…,xm) is a basis, so the dimension is m+1.
