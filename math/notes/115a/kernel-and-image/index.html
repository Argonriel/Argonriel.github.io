MATH 115A · Linear Algebra I

Kernel and image

deflinear-algebra/maps

Let T:V→W be linear.

ker⁡T={v∈V:T(v)=0},im⁡T={T(v):v∈V}. Both are subspaces, of V and W respectively.

Injectivity and surjectivity

T injective⟺ker⁡T={0}, T surjective⟺im⁡T=W.

For injectivity, T(u)=T(v) is equivalent to T(u−v)=0.

Solving T(x)=b: a solution exists iff b∈im⁡T. If x0 is one solution, every solution is x0+ker⁡T={x0+z:z∈ker⁡T}. Thus a nonhomogeneous solution set is generally an affine translate of the kernel.

For A∈Mm×n(F), im⁡A is its column space, rank⁡A=dim⁡im⁡A, and nullity⁡A=dim⁡ker⁡A.

The quotient V/ker⁡T removes precisely the ambiguity among preimages; see Quotient space.
