MATH 115A · Linear Algebra I

Subspace

deflinear-algebra/foundations

Let V be a vector space over F.

Subspace test

A subset W⊆V is a subspace if 0∈W,au+bv∈W(u,v∈W, a,b∈F).

The operations are inherited from V; the remaining vector-space axioms need not be checked again.

Examples: a homogeneous solution set {x:Ax=0}, polynomials of degree at most m (including 0), and continuous functions on a fixed interval.

Counterexample: {(x,y):x+y=1} is an affine line, not a subspace: it does not contain 0.

Intersections of subspaces are subspaces. A union U∪W is a subspace iff one of U,W contains the other. The two coordinate axes in R2 show what can fail under addition.

The smallest subspace containing a set S is its span.
