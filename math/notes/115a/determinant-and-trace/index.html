MATH 115A · Linear Algebra I

Determinant and trace

deflinear-algebra/operators

For A∈Mn(F), tr⁡A=∑i=1naii. The determinant is alternating and multilinear in the rows (also in the columns), normalized by detI=1.

Basic identities: det(AB)=detAdetB,detAT=detA, A invertible⟺detA≠0,detA−1=(detA)−1. The inverse formula assumes A is invertible. Cofactor expansion along row i is detA=∑j=1n(−1)i+jaijdetAi^,j^.

Trace is linear; determinant is generally not linear as a function of the whole matrix. For example, det(2In)=2n over R, not 2 when n>1.

The identity tr⁡(AB)=tr⁡(BA) and multiplicativity of determinant show both are invariant under similarity. They therefore define tr⁡T and detT independently of a basis.

If the characteristic polynomial splits over F, list its roots λ1,…,λn with algebraic multiplicity. Then tr⁡A=∑jλj,detA=∏jλj. Splitting over C does not make the roots real.
