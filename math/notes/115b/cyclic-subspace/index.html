MATH 115B · Linear Algebra II

Cyclic subspace

deflinear-algebra/operators

Let T∈L(V) with V finite-dimensional. The cyclic subspace generated by v is Z(v,T)=span⁡{v,Tv,T2v,…}. It is the smallest T-invariant subspace containing v.

If v≠0, let r be the first index for which Trv lies in the span of its predecessors. Then β=(v,Tv,…,Tr−1v) is a basis of Z(v,T), and there are unique a0,…,ar−1 such that Trv=∑j=0r−1ajTjv.

Companion matrix

In this basis, T|Z(v,T) has ones immediately below the diagonal, last column (a0,…,ar−1)T, and all other entries zero.

Its characteristic and minimal polynomials are both p(t)=tr−ar−1tr−1−⋯−a1t−a0. The characteristic polynomial follows by expanding the companion determinant. Also p(T)v=0, so p(T) kills every Tjv; a smaller-degree relation would contradict independence of β.

If Z(v,T)=V, then v is a cyclic vector for T. Not every operator has one: I on a space of dimension greater than 1 generates only a line from each nonzero vector.
