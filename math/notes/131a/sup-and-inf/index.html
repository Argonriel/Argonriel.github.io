MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Sup & inf

defanalysis/completeness

Let ∅≠A⊆R.

Definition

s=supA means ∀a∈A: a≤s,(∀a∈A: a≤u)⟹s≤u. t=infA means ∀a∈A: t≤a,(∀a∈A: ℓ≤a)⟹ℓ≤t.

Existence in R: bounded above ⟹ finite supA; bounded below ⟹ finite infA. This is the least-upper-bound form of completeness.

Epsilon characterization

s=supA⟺{a≤s∀a∈A,∀ε>0 ∃a∈A: s−ε<a.

Example: A=(0,1) has infA=0, supA=1, but no minimum or maximum. Sup/inf need not belong to A.
