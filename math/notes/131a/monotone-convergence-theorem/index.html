MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Monotone Convergence Theorem

thmanalysis/sequences

Theorem (real sequences)

Nondecreasing and bounded above ⟹xn→supnxn. Nonincreasing and bounded below ⟹xn→infnxn.

Proof (nondecreasing): put L=supnxn. Given ε>0, choose N with L−ε<xN. Then n≥N⟹L−ε<xN≤xn≤L⟹|xn−L|<ε.

Unbounded case: nondecreasing and unbounded above ⟹xn→+∞; nonincreasing and unbounded below ⟹xn→−∞. Thus a monotone real sequence always has an extended-real limit.
