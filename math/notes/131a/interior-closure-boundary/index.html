MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Interior, closure, boundary

defanalysis/topology

Let E⊆X in a metric space (X,d); all complements and balls are relative to X.

Definitions

E∘={x∈E:∃r>0, BX(x,r)⊆E}, E―={x∈X:∀r>0, BX(x,r)∩E≠∅}, ∂E=E―∖E∘.

E― is also the intersection of all closed subsets of X containing E. A boundary point has every ball meeting both E and X∖E.

Identities

  • E open ⟺E=E∘.
  • E closed ⟺E=E―.
  • ∂E=E―∩X∖E―, hence the boundary is closed.
  • E―=E∘∪∂E (disjoint union).

Example in R: for E=(0,1], E∘=(0,1), E―=[0,1], ∂E={0,1}.
