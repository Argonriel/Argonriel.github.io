MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Alternating Series Test

thmanalysis/series

Theorem (Leibniz)

If an≥0, an+1≤an, and an→0, then ∑n=1∞(−1)nanconverges.

Proof idea: the even and odd partial sums are monotone in opposite directions, bound each other, and differ by a term tending to 0.

Remainder estimate: if S is the sum and SN the Nth partial sum, |S−SN|≤aN+1.

Example: ∑(−1)n/n converges conditionally. The test does not decide absolute convergence.
