MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Cauchy criterion for series

thmanalysis/series

Theorem

For a real or complex series ∑an, ∑an converges⟺∀ε>0 ∃N ∀m>n≥N:|∑i=n+1mai|<ε.

Proof: the displayed tail is |Sm−Sn| for partial sums Sn. Apply “Cauchy iff convergent” in R or C.

Use: prove convergence without computing the sum. Small individual terms are insufficient; all sufficiently late finite tails must be small.
