MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Integral test

thmanalysis/series

Theorem

If f:[1,∞)→[0,∞) is continuous and nonincreasing, then ∑n=1∞f(n)<∞⟺∫1∞f(x)dx<∞.

Comparison by rectangles: for N≥1, ∫1N+1f(x)dx≤∑n=1Nf(n)≤f(1)+∫1Nf(x)dx.

Application: ∑1/np converges iff p>1. For p>0, apply the test to x−p; for p≤0, the terms do not tend to 0.

The monotonicity hypothesis is essential to this rectangle comparison; positivity and continuity alone are insufficient.
