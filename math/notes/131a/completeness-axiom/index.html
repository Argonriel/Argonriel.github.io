MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Completeness Axiom

axiomanalysis/completeness

Course axiom

Every Cauchy sequence in R converges to a point of R.

131A takes this as an axiom. If R is first constructed using Dedekind cuts, completeness can instead be proved for that construction.

Equivalent formulations for R (with its usual ordered-field structure and Archimedean property):

  1. Every Cauchy sequence converges in R.
  2. Every nonempty subset bounded above has a least upper bound in R.
  3. Every bounded monotone real sequence converges.

Why Q fails: rational decimal truncations 1,1.4,1.41,1.414,… of 2 are Cauchy, but their limit is not rational.
