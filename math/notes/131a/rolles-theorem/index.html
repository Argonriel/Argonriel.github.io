MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Rolle's Theorem

thmanalysis/differentiation

Theorem

Let a<b, f∈C([a,b]), and suppose f is differentiable on (a,b) with f(a)=f(b). Then ∃c∈(a,b):f′(c)=0.

Proof: by Extreme Value Theorem, f attains max and min. If f is constant, any c∈(a,b) works. Otherwise, since the endpoint values agree, at least one extremum occurs in the interior; apply Fermat's theorem.

Rolle is the f(a)=f(b) special case of MVT, and supplies its standard proof.
