MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Nested Closed Sets Theorem

thmanalysis/topology

Theorem

Let F1⊇F2⊇⋯ be nonempty closed subsets of Rd, with F1 bounded. Then ⋂n=1∞Fn≠∅.

Proof: choose xn∈Fn⊆F1. By Bolzano–Weierstrass Theorem, xnk→x. For each fixed m, eventually nk≥m, so xnk∈Fm; closedness gives x∈Fm. Thus x∈⋂mFm.

Hypotheses matter

  • Without boundedness: Fn=[n,∞) have empty intersection.
  • Without closedness: Fn=(0,1/n) have empty intersection.

Metric-space version: a decreasing sequence of nonempty compact sets has nonempty intersection. More generally, use nonempty closed subsets of one fixed compact set. “Closed and bounded” alone does not supply compactness in an arbitrary metric space.
