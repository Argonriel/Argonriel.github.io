MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Partial sums

defanalysis/series

Definition

For a real sequence (an), put Sn=∑i=1nai. ∑n=1∞an=S∈R⟺Sn→S.

If an≥0, then (Sn) is nondecreasing. By Monotone Convergence Theorem, the series converges to a finite sum iff its partial sums are bounded above; otherwise Sn→+∞.

Necessary, not sufficient

∑an converges⟹an=Sn−Sn−1→0. The converse fails: ∑1/n diverges.
