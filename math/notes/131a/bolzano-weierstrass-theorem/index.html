MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Bolzano–Weierstrass Theorem

thmanalysis/sequences

Theorem

Every bounded sequence in Rd has a convergent subsequence.

Proof in R: choose a monotone subsequence, then apply Monotone Convergence Theorem. In finite-dimensional Rd, successively extract subsequences for each coordinate.

Where is the limit? A bounded sequence in E has a subsequence converging somewhere in Rd. If E is also closed, the limit lies in E.

E⊆Rd closed and bounded⟹every sequence in E has a convergent subsequence with limit in E. This is the sequential form of compactness. It resolves the original “?bdd???”: boundedness extracts a limit; closedness keeps it inside E.

Counterexample: xn=1/n∈(0,1] converges to 0∉(0,1].
