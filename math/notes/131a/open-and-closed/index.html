MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Open & closed

defanalysis/topology

Work in an ambient metric space (X,d); in 131A, X=R or Rd.

Definitions

U⊆X is open if ∀x∈U ∃r>0: BX(x,r)⊆U. F⊆X is closed if X∖F is open. Clopen means both; X and ∅ are clopen.

Sequential characterization

F closed in X⟺[xn∈F, xn→x in X⟹x∈F].

If X is complete, this also says every Cauchy sequence in F converges in F. Without completeness the Cauchy formulation fails: Q is closed in itself but is not complete.

Stability under unions and intersections

Operation Open sets Closed sets
Arbitrary union Open Not necessarily closed
Finite union Open Closed
Arbitrary intersection Not necessarily open Closed
Finite intersection Open Closed
