MATH 131A · Real Analysis I

Comparison test

thmanalysis/series

Theorem (nonnegative terms)

Suppose 0≤bn≤an for all sufficiently large n.

  • ∑an convergent ⟹∑bn convergent.
  • ∑bn divergent ⟹∑an divergent.

Reason: nonnegative partial sums are monotone; the inequality transfers an upper bound or forces divergence to +∞.

Absolute comparison

If |bn|≤an eventually and ∑an converges, then ∑bn converges absolutely, hence converges.

Changing finitely many terms does not affect convergence. For signed terms, bn≤an alone is not the absolute comparison hypothesis.
