MATH 131B · Real Analysis II

Fourier coefficients

defanalysis/fourier

Work with f∈C(T,C), T=R/Z, and the inner product in L2 space.

Modes and coefficients

en(x)=e2πinx,n∈Z, ⟨en,em⟩=∫01e2πi(n−m)xdx=δnm, f^(n)=⟨f,en⟩=∫01f(x)e−2πinxdx.

f^(n) is the component of f in frequency n.

Orthogonal projection

Let VN=span⁡{e−N,…,eN} and FN(x)=∑n=−NNf^(n)en(x). Then f−FN is orthogonal to VN. For every P∈VN, ‖f−P‖22=‖f−FN‖22+‖FN−P‖22, so FN is the best L2 approximation in VN.

This finite projection identity does not assert uniform convergence of FN. Fourier theorem gives uniform approximation by Fejér means; Parseval identity explains L2 convergence of the ordinary partial sums.
