MATH 131B · Real Analysis II

Riemann integral

defthmanalysis/integration

Let a<b be finite and f:[a,b]→R bounded. For a partition P:a=x0<⋯<xm=b, put L(f,P)=∑j=1m(inf[xj−1,xj]f)(xj−xj−1), U(f,P)=∑j=1m(sup[xj−1,xj]f)(xj−xj−1).

Definition and criterion

f Riemann integrable⟺supPL(f,P)=infPU(f,P)=:∫abf. Equivalently, ∀ε>0 ∃P: U(f,P)−L(f,P)<ε.

Sufficient conditions: continuous or monotone on [a,b] implies Riemann integrable. Products and absolute values of Riemann-integrable functions are integrable.

Basic estimate: |∫abf|≤∫ab|f|≤(b−a)‖f‖∞.

Cauchy–Schwarz: for Riemann-integrable real or complex f,g, ∫ab|fg|≤(∫ab|f|2)1/2(∫ab|g|2)1/2. For complex functions, integrability means integrability of real and imaginary parts. The absolute squares are necessary in the complex form used in L2 space.
