Weierstrass M-test

thmanalysis/functions

Theorem

Let fn:X→R or C, where X≠∅. If |fn(x)|≤Mn∀x∈X,Mn≥0,∑n=1∞Mn<∞, then both ∑fn and ∑|fn| converge uniformly on X.

Proof by tails: for m>n, supx∈X|∑k=n+1mfk(x)|≤∑k=n+1mMk≤∑k>nMk→0. The partial sums are sup-Cauchy; use completeness of bounded functions. The same bound applies to the series of absolute values.

Convenient form: ∑n‖fn‖∞<∞ is sufficient for uniform convergence.

Not necessary: constant functions fn(x)=(−1)n/n form a uniformly convergent series (its numerical tails are independent of x), but ∑‖fn‖∞=∞.
