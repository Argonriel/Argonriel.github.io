MATH 131B · Real Analysis II

Spaces of functions

defanalysis/functions

For a finite interval [a,b] with a<b, consider real-valued functions:

Space Meaning
B([a,b]) Bounded functions
R([a,b]) Riemann-integrable functions
C([a,b]) Continuous functions
C1([a,b]) Continuous functions, differentiable on (a,b), with derivative extending continuously to [a,b]

C1([a,b])⊂C([a,b])⊂R([a,b])⊂B([a,b]).

Sup metric: d∞(f,g)=‖f−g‖∞=supx∈[a,b]|f(x)−g(x)|. Convergence in this metric is exactly Uniform convergence within these spaces.

Completeness: B([a,b]) is complete. C([a,b]) and R([a,b]) are closed subspaces under d∞ because their properties survive uniform limits, so they are also complete.

Caution: C1([a,b]) with only the sup metric is not complete. On [−1,1], fn(x)=x2+1/n⇉|x|,0≤fn(x)−|x|≤n−1/2, but |x|∉C1([−1,1]). The metric matters; the stronger norm ‖f‖∞+‖f′‖∞ gives a different completeness statement.
