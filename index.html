Research

Finite-Time Dynamics in FPUT and Toda Lattices

January 2026–Present

Independent research following UCLA MATH 134

I study energy redistribution in Fermi–Pasta–Ulam–Tsingou (FPUT) oscillator chains using fourth-order Yoshida symplectic integration in Python. I compare spectral entropy, finite-time Lyapunov estimates, and the drift of a selected Toda integral to examine how these diagnostics describe dynamics over finite observation windows.

Current work includes numerical sensitivity checks and FPUT–Toda switching experiments with paired FPUT controls, examining how intermediate Toda evolution affects subsequent FPUT dynamics.
